\section{Methods}
Lattice tiles was folded in buffer containing \SI{12.5}{\milli\molar} MgCl$_2$ with a ten-fold staple excess \cite{alder2021,brook2022}. Samples was annealed from 90 to 20~\si{\celsius} during 16 h, and then they was purified by PEG precipitation.
Yield was quantified from AFM images (Fig.~\ref{fig:afm}) as the fraction of intact tiles, see Eq.~\eqref{eq:yield}.
\begin{equation}
  Y = \frac{N_\mathrm{intact}}{N_\mathrm{total}} \label{eq:yield}
\end{equation}
